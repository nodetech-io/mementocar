Your house keys, the groceries in the trunk, the parking ticket. Memento Car reminds you right when it matters: when you get in or out of your car.

The app detects when your phone connects to or disconnects from your car's Bluetooth and shows the reminders you set for that moment. No extra device needed: just the Bluetooth you already use in your car.

HOW IT WORKS
• Link your car once by picking its Bluetooth from your paired devices.
• Create reminders "When leaving" or "When entering".
• When your phone connects to or disconnects from the car, you get a notification with what's pending.
• Tick each item with "Complete", or use "Snooze 10 min" if now isn't a good time.

REMINDERS THAT FIT YOUR ROUTINE
• Frequency: just once, or every time you get in or out of the car.
• Choose the days of the week each reminder applies.
• Mark what really can't be missed as "Important": the alert repeats until you complete or snooze it.
• Optional alert delay: immediate, or after 1, 5, 10 or 30 minutes.
• Several cars, each with its own reminders.

HANDS-FREE DICTATION
• Create reminders by voice in English, Spanish or Catalan. For example: "buy bread when leaving".
• Before saving, review the reminder, the car and the moment, and correct them by voice.
• Use your voice with pending alerts too: "mark keys", "snooze" or "all done".
• Add the "New reminder" tile to Quick Settings to start dictating from any screen.

STAY IN CONTROL
• The "Status" tab checks permissions, your car's Bluetooth and the latest alert, and helps you send a test alert.
• Export and import a backup of your cars and reminders as a file.

FREE, NO ACCOUNT
Every feature is free: no ads, no in-app purchases and no account to create.

PRIVACY
Memento Car stores your cars and reminders on your device and has no developer-owned reminder server. Voice features are optional and use your phone's speech services, which may process data online.

Requires a car with Bluetooth paired to your phone.
